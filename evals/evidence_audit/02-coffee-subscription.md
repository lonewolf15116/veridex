We're launching a direct-to-consumer subscription box for small-batch, single-origin coffee, priced at $28/month including shipping. Our differentiation is a "roaster of the month" model — we partner with 12 independent roasters a year rather than roasting in-house. Customer acquisition is entirely through Instagram/TikTok influencer partnerships, budgeted at $40 CAC. We project 15% month-over-month subscriber growth for the first year, reaching 10,000 subscribers by month 12, and plan to raise a $2M seed round on the back of that traction to fund inventory and a second fulfillment center.

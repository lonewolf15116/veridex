Our platform connects homeowners needing small repair jobs (under $500) with independent local handymen, taking a 15% commission on each transaction. We'll bootstrap supply by manually recruiting 200 handymen in Austin through Facebook groups and Nextdoor, then use a $50 referral bonus to get them to invite other tradespeople. Demand-side acquisition is via Google Ads targeting "handyman near me" searches, at an estimated $25 CAC per homeowner. We expect to reach transaction liquidity (average 3+ jobs/week per active handyman) within 4 months in the Austin market before expanding to 3 more Texas cities.
